\section{Model Comparison}

We compare the five models in the hierarchy of Figure~\ref{fig:diagram:model_hierarchy} using a likelihood ratio test (LRT) for each edge, 
as well as comparing the AIC and BIC.

Table~\ref{tab:model_stats} reports the per-model statistics for all five
models in the hierarchy of Figure~\ref{fig:diagram:model_hierarchy}.
Table~\ref{tab:lrt_comparison} reports the LRT, $p$-value, $\Delta\text{AIC}$
and $\Delta\text{BIC}$ for each edge of the hierarchy, where the difference
is taken as expanded minus base so that negative values favour the expanded
model.
